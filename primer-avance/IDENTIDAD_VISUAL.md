## Prompt final utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original logo symbol for Sentinela Selknam, an environmental satellite monitoring application in Tierra del Fuego, Argentina. Image 1 is an anatomical reference of Ko'taix: the long lateral horns extend to the LEFT AND RIGHT, almost horizontally in a very wide T-shape, with slightly raised tips; a human oval mask and broad alternating body stripes. Image 2 is a reference for a confident watchful stance and an athletic silhouette, not an illustration to copy. Use these references to create a fresh minimal professional emblem. Essential: keep the DISTINCTIVE LONG SIDEWAYS HORIZONTAL HORNS connected above the head, NOT vertical horns, NOT a Viking helmet, NOT an owl, NOT deer antlers. Depict a compact three-quarter upper-body guardian leaning forward attentively, with one bent arm resting under the chin or across the chest, suggesting a sentinel observing the territory. Human face mask with restrained dark watchful eyes, broad smooth white and blue body stripes inspired by the references, orange mask and small orange horn tips. Colors ONLY: Tierra del Fuego flag ocean blue #076A9D, warm orange #F39B36, white, and very limited deep navy #173B53 for definition. Use an entirely new composition and simplified geometry rather than tracing the supplied artwork. Flat vector-style graphic, solid filled shapes, clean deliberate edges, strong silhouette readable at 64px, dignified institutional identity rather than sports mascot, no aggression or weapons. Center the symbol on a square artboard with 10 percent clear margins around the horizontal horns. Actually transparent background outside the symbol. No lettering, no words, no watermark, no mockup, no seal or shield border, no government coat of arms, no mountains, no gradients, no photographic textures, no 3D. The figure must have the broad horizontal head silhouette seen in the references and a compact torso suitable for a website header and footer.
